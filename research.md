### Paper 10  
**SComparative Gait and Behavioral Analysis of an Ambiguous Bipedal Video Clip in Relation to the Patterson–Gimlin Film**  
This paper examines a short, low-resolution bipedal walking clip circulating across TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook, framed as “found footage.” The analysis focuses on observable gait mechanics and a brief rearward “lookback” behavior during locomotion.

- Format: PDF / DOCX
