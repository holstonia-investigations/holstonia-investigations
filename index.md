# Holstonia Bigfoot Investigations

Holstonia Bigfoot Investigations is an independent research initiative examining anomalous biological reports through standardized field methods, ecological modeling, and comparative ethology. The project does not presume conclusions and emphasizes transparency, methodological rigor, and clearly stated uncertainty.

## What This Project Is

- A structured, method-driven examination of reported biological anomalies
- A public archive of research papers, reference materials, and analytical frameworks
- An effort to move discussion away from speculation and toward testable inference

## What This Project Is Not

- A platform for sensational claims or entertainment
- An assertion of proof regarding unverified organisms
- A replacement for formal academic research institutions

## Scope of Work

Current work includes:
- Presence-only modeling and observer-effort analysis
- Acoustic reference libraries and triage workflows
- Comparative gait and locomotion analysis
- Paleoanthropological and ecological context review

## A Note on the Name "Holstonia"

The name *Holstonia* is used here in honor of the late Dr. James O. Glanville and his lifelong work on the archaeology and deep history of the Upper Holston region of Southwest Virginia and Northeast Tennessee. This project is independent of, and separate from, the Holstonia archival collections maintained elsewhere.
