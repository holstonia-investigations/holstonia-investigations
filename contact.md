# Contact

Holstonia Bigfoot Investigations welcomes scholarly inquiry,
methodological critique, and media questions related to the
research published on this site.

---

## General correspondence

For scholarly correspondence, methodological questions,
or media inquiries related to Holstonia Bigfoot Investigations,
please contact:

**Email:** holstoniabigfootinvestigations@gmail.com

Please include a brief description of the purpose of your inquiry
and, where relevant, reference the specific paper or material
being discussed. 
Messages unrelated to the research scope may not receive a response.

---

## Media and interview requests

Journalists or podcasters seeking clarification or interview
requests are encouraged to review the **Media & FAQ** page
prior to reaching out, as many common questions are addressed there.

---

## Scope of Contact

Please note that **Holstonia Bigfoot Investigations (HBI)** is unable to:

- Investigate individual sightings or claims  
- Evaluate private evidence outside the published framework  
- Respond to anonymous or abusive correspondence  

Reports of potential encounters should instead be submitted directly to the **Bigfoot Field Researchers Organization (BFRO)** through its public reporting system.

The author serves separately as a BFRO investigator and reviews and accepts reports submitted through that system for possible follow-up, subject to BFRO policies and procedures.
